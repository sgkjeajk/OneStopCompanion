    🎯 Exercise Difficulty

    Gameplay Level

    Each level uses genuinely different, more challenging moves within its category — not just longer timers. Easy is slow and fully supported. Medium adds a bit more range of motion. Hard reduces hand support and adds faster pace. Advanced includes single-leg balance, dual-task moves (moving while counting), and shorter rests between exercises.

    Automatic starts at Easy and moves up one level after 3 routines completed in a row without skipping any exercise. Choosing a level yourself keeps every routine at that level, no matter how many times you play.
